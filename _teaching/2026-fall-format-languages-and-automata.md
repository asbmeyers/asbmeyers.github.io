Instructor: Alexi Brooks

As a graduate teaching assistant, I lead weekly discussion sections, which consist of short independently designed review plans followed by small group work time on a set of assigned discussion problems. During this work time I still actively assess students' progress, having short conversations with students to verify proper understanding of course topics and assisting with any questions that students may have. 

I also hold weekly office hours and grade class assignments.
