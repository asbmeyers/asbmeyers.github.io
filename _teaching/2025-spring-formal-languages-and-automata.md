Instructor: Carl Sturtivant

As an undergraduate teaching assistant, I held weekly office hours, during which I would answer any questions posed by the students. During this semester, my office hours had a high repeat attendance of 5-10 students in most weeks, who I would assist through explanations of various core concepts in formal languages and automata theory. By the end of the semester, the majority of students that regularly attended my office hours received an A in the course.
