As a graduate teaching assistant, I led weekly discussion sections with independently designed presentations and lesson plans, reviewing the information taught in the previous week's lectures. In each discussion section, I walked students through a variety of explanations and examples using personally designed slides and diagrams. In addition, I made sure students took an active part in each discussion, allowing them to work through a variety of practice problems, and ask questions at any point during the class period.

In addition, I coordinated the undergraduate TAs' in grading assignments and exams, then reviewed and released the grades to the students.

I scored >5.3/6.0 on all individual Student Rating of Teaching (SRT) categories at the end of the semester, from a total of 15 responding students.
